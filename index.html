Gravity Assist

Steer a rocket through gravity wells. Grab the beacons, reach the gate, beat the clock.

Controls
Touch
Left buttons steer, BURN fires the engine. Drag and pinch before your first burn to look around.
Keyboard
Up arrow burns, left and right steer. W, A and D work too.
Mouse
Hold on the map: the ship turns toward the pointer and burns once lined up.
Keys
R restart, P pause, G orbit guide, M mute, F full screen.
Hints
Pause, then Watch the solution, to see the best route.
Load save or transfer to another device
